What helps confirm property fit

  • Use nearby comparable properties with similar condition rather than a Washington-wide average for your listing assumption.
  • Request an address-specific estimate of seller closing charges. Do not infer taxes from a county name.
  • Ask for repair or preparation scopes that fit the actual house. An urban condominium and a rural property with outbuildings need different inputs.
  • For travel, ferry access or an occupied property, put the practical time and coordination into your plan before deciding how long a listing may take.
