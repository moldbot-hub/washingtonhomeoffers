Washington seller preparation

Compare a written Washington cash offer with an agent listing

Use a decision worksheet that keeps price, uncertain estimates, transaction costs, conditions, and timing visible on the same page.

Prepared by Washington Home Solutions LLC · Reviewed September 5, 2026

Use comparable starting figures

Label each number by its source and certainty. A signed purchase proposal, a buyer’s preliminary range, and an agent’s suggested list price are different kinds of information. Record the date, property condition assumed, and any inspection still outstanding. Do not compare an optimistic asking price with a firm offer as though both were guaranteed proceeds.

Build the net-proceeds comparison

For each path, begin with the applicable sale-price figure. Then list quoted commissions or service fees, seller-paid transaction costs, agreed repairs or concessions, and estimated holding costs through the proposed closing. Keep mortgage payoff and other property obligations visible. Obtain current figures from the appropriate parties; this worksheet does not calculate your taxes or establish a final settlement statement.

Put timing and conditions beside the numbers

Record the proposed closing and possession dates, financing conditions, inspection rights, outstanding approvals, and what could change the price or stop the transaction. A short advertised timeline is less useful than a written schedule whose dependencies are understood. Ask who is responsible for the next step and when you should expect confirmation.

Account for the work each path requires

List preparation, cleaning, showings, travel, document gathering, and access coordination. Distinguish tasks you would do yourself from services you would pay for. A house can be suitable for a listing even if a direct offer feels simpler, and a direct purchase can be useful when the proposed terms fit the owner’s priorities.

Make the decision from the complete proposal

Review the actual agreement and ask qualified help about terms you do not understand. Washington Home Solutions LLC operates Washington Home Offers and may benefit if it purchases your property.
